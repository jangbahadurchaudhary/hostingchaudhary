Geopolitics & US Politics

Trump Defies Supreme Court with 15% Global Tariff Hike Amid Mar-a-Lago Security Breach

The global economic landscape was thrown into chaos this weekend following a dramatic series of moves by U.S. President Donald Trump. Just one day after the U.S. Supreme Court struck down his sweeping use of emergency powers to impose tariffs on foreign imports, Trump doubled down, announcing a massive 15% blanket tariff on all foreign goods. Legal experts note that Trump is now attempting to invoke older, more obscure trade statutes to bypass the Supreme Court's ruling.

The 15% levy, which revises a 10% duty he had declared just hours after the court's decision, has sent shockwaves through global markets. U.S. Trade Representative Jamieson Greer is concurrently preparing to travel to India next month to finalize the first tranche of an India-U.S. trade deal, but international partners remain on edge. The sudden tariff chaos comes just ahead of a highly anticipated summit between Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping, giving Xi renewed leverage in trade negotiations.

"The terms of the framework for any interim agreement must be renegotiated in light of the confusion following the American Supreme Court's verdict striking down the President's global tariffs." — Jairam Ramesh, Congress General Secretary in-charge of communications.

Security Breach at Mar-a-Lago

Compounding the tense political weekend, a fatal security incident occurred at President Trump's Mar-a-Lago estate in Florida. U.S. Secret Service agents shot and killed a man attempting to unlawfully enter the resort's perimeter. According to official reports, the suspect was observed at the north gate around 1:30 a.m. EST carrying what appeared to be a shotgun and a fuel can. Investigations are currently underway to determine the intruder's motives.

India Politics & Infrastructure

PM Modi Flags Off Meerut Metro, Slams Congress Protest at AI Summit

On Sunday, Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated a massive infrastructure upgrade in Uttar Pradesh, officially flagging off the Meerut Metro and dedicating the 82-kilometre Delhi–Meerut Namo Bharat Corridor to the nation. The ₹12,930 crore project represents a massive leap in high-speed intercity travel within the National Capital Region (NCR). The Prime Minister undertook a metro ride from Shatabdi Nagar to Meerut South Station, interacting with students and daily commuters.

However, the inauguration rally quickly turned highly political. PM Modi used the platform to fiercely criticize the opposition Congress party for organizing a "shirtless" protest outside the ongoing global Artificial Intelligence Impact Summit in New Delhi. The Prime Minister accused the opposition of tarnishing India's global image.

Additionally, during his monthly radio address Mann Ki Baat, PM Modi announced a highly symbolic move regarding India's colonial past: the statue of British architect Edwin Lutyens at Rashtrapati Bhavan will be removed and replaced with a statue of C. Rajagopalachari, independent India's first Governor-General.

Global Conflicts

Cross-Border Strikes Escalate Tensions in South Asia and Eastern Europe

Military escalations have dominated the weekend across two major global fault lines. In South Asia, Pakistan confirmed it carried out intelligence-based airstrikes inside Afghanistan, specifically targeting militant positions in the Behsud district of Nangarhar province. The Pakistani military claims these strikes were aimed at hideouts of the Tehrik-e-Taliban Pakistan (TTP), who they blame for a recent wave of suicide bombings. Reports indicate at least 17 people were killed, drawing furious condemnation from Kabul, who warned of a "measured response."

Meanwhile, in Eastern Europe, the grueling conflict between Russia and Ukraine saw intense weekend exchanges. Russian forces launched a barrage of drones and missiles at the suburbs of Kyiv, resulting in at least one civilian death. In a swift retaliation, Ukrainian forces successfully struck a crucial ballistic missile production facility deep inside Russia's Udmurt region, dealing a heavy blow to Moscow's industrial weapons manufacturing capabilities.

Business & Science

Adani Expands Power Projects; NASA Delays Artemis II Moon Mission

In domestic business news, Gautam Adani, Chairman of the Adani Group, conducted high-level reviews of mega power projects across Eastern India. He inspected the 1,600 MW ultra-supercritical thermal power plant in Godda, Jharkhand—a ₹16,000 crore facility that exclusively supplies electricity to Bangladesh. Adani also announced plans for a new 2,400 MW power project at Pirpainti near Bhagalpur, Bihar, requiring an estimated investment of ₹27,000 crore to meet rising urban energy demands.

In aerospace news, humanity's highly anticipated return to the moon has faced a technical setback. NASA officially announced the delay of the Artemis II mission, which is designed to send four astronauts around the Moon. The space agency cited a critical interruption in helium flow within the Space Launch System's (SLS) interim cryogenic propulsion stage as the reason for the delay, prioritizing crew safety over mission timelines.
