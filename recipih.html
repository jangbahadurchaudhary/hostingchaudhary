Delicious, viral, and easy to make

🍫 Olympic Chocolate Muffins

Inspired by the Paris 2024 Olympic Village — rich, fluffy, and chocolate-packed.

Ingredients:

  • 1½ cups flour
  • ½ cup cocoa powder
  • 2 eggs, ¾ cup sugar
  • ½ cup yogurt, ½ cup oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla, ½ cup chocolate chips

Instructions:

Mix dry and wet separately, combine, bake at 180°C for 18–20 mins.

🍡 Tanghulu: TikTok’s Glossy Fruit Candy

Crunchy, colorful, and perfect for reels — Tanghulu is the viral snack of the year.

Ingredients:

  • Strawberries, grapes, kiwi
  • 1 cup sugar, ½ cup water
  • Skewers

Instructions:

Boil sugar to 150°C, dip fruits, cool on parchment. Snap-worthy!

🧀 Tini’s Viral Mac & Cheese

Loaded with cheese and nostalgia — the ultimate comfort food of 2025.

Ingredients:

  • Cheddar, mozzarella, cream cheese
  • Butter, flour, milk
  • Elbow macaroni, paprika

Instructions:

Make roux, melt cheese, mix with pasta, bake at 180°C for 15 mins.
